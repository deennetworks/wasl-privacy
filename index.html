Wasl — Política de privacidad

Última actualización: 21 de noviembre de 2026

Español · English · Français · العربية

Wasl (وصل) es una aplicación del Corán y de los horarios de rezo creada por Deen Networks. Esta política explica de forma directa qué datos usa la app y qué se hace con ellos.

El resumen: Wasl no muestra anuncios y no usa analítica ni rastreadores. Por defecto, todo lo que la app guarda se queda en tu dispositivo. Solo sale algo de él en tres casos que eliges tú: si inicias sesión con Google en Android (una cuenta opcional para sincronizar tus rezos y marcadores), si activas las notificaciones push del iPhone (OneSignal) y al usar la recitación del Corán, cuyo catálogo y audio se piden a Quran Foundation a través de un servidor de Wasl. En iPhone, la cuenta opcional con Iniciar sesión con Apple se guarda en el llavero del teléfono y no se envía a ningún servidor. Todo se explica más abajo.

Cómo funciona (y por qué casi no hay nada que recopilar)

  • Wasl funciona en tu teléfono: los horarios de rezo, la dirección de la Qibla y tu historial se calculan y se guardan ahí.
  • El texto del Corán y sus traducciones vienen incluidos en la app: no se descargan de ningún servidor.
  • La recitación del Corán es la excepción: al abrir la lista de recitadores o al pulsar play, la app pide a un servidor de Wasl (alojado en Cloudflare) el catálogo y las URLs de audio, y reproduce el audio en streaming desde Quran Foundation. Esas peticiones llevan datos técnicos de la conexión (dirección IP, dispositivo y sura solicitada), pero no tu cuenta, ubicación, historial ni marcadores.
  • La versión de Android usa Internet solo para la cuenta opcional; la recitación está en la versión de iPhone.
  • Los horarios de rezo y la dirección de la Qibla se calculan en el propio dispositivo, a partir de tu ubicación o de la ciudad que elijas.

Cuenta opcional en iPhone (Iniciar sesión con Apple)

En la primera apertura, la app ofrece iniciar sesión con Apple; el botón Omitir está visible y la app funciona entera sin cuenta. Si entras, la app guarda en el llavero del iPhone el identificador estable de Apple y, si lo autorizas, tu nombre y tu correo. La cuenta no se envía a ningún servidor: el servidor de Wasl solo sirve el catálogo y las URLs de recitación y no recibe datos de la cuenta. No se guardan tokens de Apple y esa información no se usa para publicidad, analítica ni para vincularla al push.

  • La cuenta no da acceso a ninguna función: sirve para mostrar quién ha iniciado sesión y poder eliminarla.
  • Se guarda con AfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly: no se sincroniza con iCloud y no sale del teléfono.
  • Si retiras el acceso de Wasl a tu Apple ID desde los Ajustes del iPhone, la app cierra la sesión; tus rezos, historial, lectura y ajustes se quedan.
  • Para eliminar la cuenta, abre Ajustes → icono de persona (arriba a la derecha) → Eliminar cuenta. Se borran la identidad y todos los datos de Wasl en este iPhone (historial, marcas, progreso, marcadores y ajustes) de forma inmediata, con confirmación.

Wasl — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Español · English · Français · العربية

Wasl (وصل) is a Quran and prayer-times app made by Deen Networks. This policy explains plainly what data the app uses and what happens to it.

The short version: Wasl shows no ads and uses no analytics or trackers. By default, everything the app saves stays on your device. Something leaves it only in three cases you choose: if you sign in with Google on Android (an optional account to sync your prayers and bookmarks), if you enable iPhone push notifications (OneSignal), and when you use Quran recitation, whose catalogue and audio are requested from Quran Foundation through a Wasl server. On iPhone, the optional Sign in with Apple account is stored in the phone's Keychain and is never sent to a server. Everything is explained below.

How it works (and why there is almost nothing to collect)

  • Wasl runs on your phone: prayer times, the Qibla direction and your history are calculated and stored there.
  • The Quran text and its translations are included in the app: nothing is downloaded from a server.
  • Quran recitation is the exception: when you open the reciter list or press play, the app asks a Wasl server (hosted on Cloudflare) for the catalogue and the audio URLs, and streams the audio directly from Quran Foundation. Those requests carry technical connection data (IP address, device and requested surah), but not your account, location, history or bookmarks.
  • The Android version uses the Internet only for the optional account; recitation is available in the iPhone version.
  • Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on the device itself, from your location or the city you choose.

Quran recitation (audio)

Recitation audio is requested only when you start it, by pressing play or "Listen from here". The app requests the reciter catalogue when you open the picker and, during playback, asks a Wasl server (hosted on Cloudflare) for the audio address of each verse; the audio is streamed directly from Quran Foundation (quran.foundation) and its CDNs.

They do not receive your account, location, prayer history, reading progress or bookmarks, and this data is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling.
  • Wasl does not store the audio or a listening history. As with any streaming playback, the device may temporarily keep part of it in memory to avoid interruptions; it is not kept as a file.
  • Without an Internet connection, recitation is not available; the rest of the app keeps working.

Optional iPhone account (Sign in with Apple)

On first launch the app offers Sign in with Apple; the Skip button is visible and the app works fully without an account. If you sign in, the app stores the stable Apple identifier and, if you authorize it, your name and email in the iPhone Keychain. The account is not sent to any server: the Wasl server only serves the catalogue and recitation URLs and receives no account data. No Apple tokens are stored, and that information is not used for advertising, analytics or linked to push.

  • The account does not unlock any feature: it shows who is signed in and allows deletion.
  • It is stored with AfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly: it is not synced to iCloud and does not leave the phone.
  • If you remove Wasl's access to your Apple ID in iPhone Settings, the app signs out; your prayers, history, reading and settings stay.
  • To delete the account, open Settings → person icon (top right) → Delete account. The identity and all Wasl data on this iPhone (history, marks, reading progress, bookmarks and settings) are erased immediately, with confirmation.

Optional account and sync (Android)

Since version 0.3.0, the Android app lets you, if you want, sign in with your Google account to keep a copy of some data in the cloud and get it back on another phone. Without an account, the app works exactly the same and sends nothing.

Push notifications on iPhone

The iPhone version can receive push notifications (notices the Wasl administrator sends from outside the app, for example an announcement). Delivery is handled by OneSignal, a notification service, through Apple's push system (APNs):

  • When you enable notifications, your device registers with OneSignal with a subscription identifier (derived from the identifier Apple assigns). That is the only thing sent: no name, no email, no account, and it is not linked to your identity.
  • That identifier is used only to deliver the app's notifications. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or tracking you.
  • Prayer reminders do not go through OneSignal: your own iPhone schedules them (local notifications) and they are computed on the device.
  • If you do not want any of this, simply do not grant the notification permission: the app works the same.

What Wasl does not do

  • No account required: on iPhone the Apple account is optional and local; on Android the Google account is optional. The app works the same without one.
  • No ads, analytics or trackers.
  • No selling of data and no use of it for advertising or tracking. The only third parties involved are technical providers: Apple for Sign in with Apple (authentication only; the data stays on the phone), Google Firebase for the optional Android account, OneSignal for iPhone push notifications, Quran Foundation for recitation audio and Cloudflare for hosting the Wasl server.
  • No profiling and no automated decisions about you.

Device backups

If you have system backups enabled (your Google account on Android, iCloud on iPhone), the app's data may be included in that backup. That is a feature of your phone: it is handled by your provider (Google or Apple) and controlled from your device settings. The developer has no access to it.

On iPhone

On iPhone, everything the app stores is calculated and stored on the device. The optional Sign in with Apple account also stays in the phone's Keychain and is never sent to a server; it is deleted in Settings → person icon → Delete account, together with the app's data on this iPhone. Recitation uses the Internet as explained above. When you use your location, Apple's system may translate the coordinates into a place name and time zone using its maps service; that lookup is made by the operating system under Apple's privacy policy, and Wasl does not receive or store it. Push notifications are explained above.

Your data stays with you

  • Uninstalling the app erases everything it stored (and the push registration is dropped); you can also clear its data from your phone settings. What the optional Android account stored in the cloud is not erased by uninstalling: to erase it, delete the account.
  • The account can be deleted from the app (Settings → person icon → Delete account). On Android this also deletes the cloud copy; on iPhone there is no cloud copy. You can also ask by email: see Delete your account.
  • The Wasl server does not keep a recitation history linked to you; it only serves the catalogue and the audio URLs.
  • For any other request about your data (access, correction), write to the contact address.

Children

Wasl is suitable for all audiences. It asks for no data in order to work; the account is optional.

Notifications push sur iPhone

La version iPhone peut recevoir des notifications push (des avis que l'administrateur de Wasl envoie depuis l'extérieur de l'app, par exemple une annonce). La livraison est assurée par OneSignal, un service de notifications, via le système de notifications d'Apple (APNs) :

  • Lorsque tu actives les notifications, ton appareil s'enregistre auprès de OneSignal avec un identifiant d'abonnement (dérivé de l'identifiant attribué par Apple). C'est la seule chose envoyée : ni nom, ni e-mail, ni compte, et il n'est pas lié à ton identité.
  • Cet identifiant sert uniquement à livrer les notifications de l'app. Il n'est utilisé ni pour la publicité, ni pour établir des profils, ni pour te suivre.
  • Les rappels de prière ne passent pas par OneSignal : ton propre iPhone les programme (notifications locales) et ils sont calculés sur l'appareil.
  • Si tu ne veux rien de tout cela, il suffit de ne pas accorder l'autorisation de notifications : l'app fonctionne de la même manière.

Ce que Wasl ne fait pas

  • Aucun compte obligatoire : sur iPhone, le compte Apple est facultatif et local ; sur Android, le compte Google est facultatif. L'app fonctionne de la même manière sans compte.
  • Aucune publicité, aucune analyse d'audience, aucun traceur.
  • Aucune vente de données et aucune utilisation à des fins de publicité ou de suivi. Les seuls tiers impliqués sont des prestataires techniques : Apple pour Se connecter avec Apple (authentification uniquement ; les données restent sur le téléphone), Google Firebase pour le compte facultatif Android, OneSignal pour les notifications push de l'iPhone, Quran Foundation pour l'audio de la récitation et Cloudflare pour l'hébergement du serveur de Wasl.
  • Aucun profilage et aucune décision automatisée te concernant.

C'est une fonction du téléphone : elle est gérée par ton fournisseur (Google ou Apple) et contrôlée depuis les réglages de l'appareil. Le développeur n'y a pas accès.

كيف يعمل التطبيق (ولماذا لا يوجد تقريبًا ما يُجمع)

  • يعمل Wasl على هاتفك: تُحسب مواقيت الصلاة واتجاه القبلة وسجلك وتُحفظ على الجهاز.
  • نص القرآن وترجماته مضمنة في التطبيق: لا تُنزَّل من أي خادم.
